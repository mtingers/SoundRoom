# Twinkle Star · darkwave

The traditional public-domain Twinkle Twinkle Little Star melody, arranged in D minor as 1980s darkwave at 100 BPM. Approximately 2:44, with seven tracks and 48 editable instrument/drum clips: detuned saw pads, glass bells, a saw lead, pulse bass, arpeggio, drums, and piano.

Open `Twinkle.soundroom` through Soundroom's **File → Open Session**, or play [Twinkle.m4a](Twinkle.m4a). The project contains all 48 rendered audio sources. Keep its `Audio` directory and `session.json` together when moving it.

The arrangement moves through intro, verse, chorus, piano break, second verse, and outro. Notes and drum patterns remain editable; recorded piano and drum parts can change sound when regenerated without the original recorded libraries.
