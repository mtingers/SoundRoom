# Nutshell · instrumental demo

The later steel-string version of the Soundroom instrumental rendition associated with Nutshell by Alice in Chains (1994). Open-chord guitars tuned down a half step, sounding E-flat minor at 68 BPM, with seven tracks and 71 editable instrument/drum clips.

Open `Nutshell.soundroom` through Soundroom's **File → Open Session**, or play [Nutshell.m4a](Nutshell.m4a). All 71 rendered audio sources are embedded, so playback does not require installing the recorded libraries or any Python models. Keep `session.json` and the `Audio` directory together.

Parts include fingerpicked and strummed steel-string acoustics, picked bass, cello, recorded drums, a string-section pad, and clean electric lead with bends and vibrato. The arrangement's melody and solo were newly written; no lyrics, vocals, or audio from the original recording are included.

The steel-string guitar is the separately prepared FreePats variant, rather than Soundroom's default recorded acoustic guitar. Regenerating those notes with a different installed library can change their sound. Save As before experimenting.
