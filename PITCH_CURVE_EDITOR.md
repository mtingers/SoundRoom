# Pitch curve editor

## Editing

1. Select a clip or audio range and choose Pitch correction. Analyze while stopped; analysis does not change audio or add an Undo entry. With nothing analyzed, the editor lists the three steps.
2. Blue (**Sung**) shows the measured input. The initial orange (**Target**) curve is a draft that follows what was sung. Pick a style or drag a point to activate it as a saved live insert. New editor inserts process only edited voiced regions, before the rest of the track chain. Existing inserts keep their original chain position and automatic processing outside edited regions.
3. The style row — Natural, Tight, Robotic and As sung — rebuilds every target in the selection from the measured input as one Undo step, and stays highlighted until a manual edit. Drag a point to move it in time and pitch; Shift-click selects several, dragging empty space selects a time range and double-click adds a point. Groups move from their original snapshot; anchors cannot cross unselected neighbors or leave their voiced region. Targets do not bridge unvoiced gaps. The snap toggle rounds dragged notes to semitones, and the trash button deletes selected points.
4. **Fine-tune** opens exact time, note and cents controls, the outgoing Hold, Linear, Smooth, Ease In or Ease Out curve, and local smoothing. Smoothing changes selected interior targets while preserving region endpoints and timing.
5. Pinch or ⌘-scroll zooms time around the pointer, sideways (or Shift) scrolling pans time and ⌥-scroll pans pitch; plain vertical scrolling still scrolls the inspector. Fit, the zoom buttons and the view menu (zoom to selected points, pitch zoom and pan) do the same explicitly; edits never change the viewport. Time zoom reaches 10 ms; the fine pitch grid reaches 25 cents. Expand opens the same bindings in a scrolling precision sheet, where Space listens/stops, Delete removes selected points, arrows nudge them (a semitone or 1 ms; ⌥ for 10 cents or 10 ms), ⌘A selects all, Esc clears the selection and Command-Z / Shift-Command-Z undo and redo. Text fields retain native text editing.
6. **More → Measure result** independently measures the rendered pitch-stage output without playing. Green (**Result**) is measured output, not a renamed requested curve. **Original** auditions this track with the edited pitch insert removed; **Listen** includes all its inserts and volume/pan automation. The main loop does not turn these one-shot auditions into a loop.

Drawn targets are followed as drawn: the corrector applies only a 4 ms jitter smoothing between grains, without the automatic mode's retune glide, vibrato retention, humanize or tolerance, so a flat Tight line comes out flat and a Natural curve keeps exactly the vibrato it contains. Retune, Humanize and Vibrato shape automatic correction only.

Natural moves measured targets halfway toward permitted notes; Tight fully snaps with linear curves; Robotic uses held snapped notes; Keep performance restores measured targets. Snap presets honor Key/Scale/custom pitch classes. Seeded presets stabilize each voiced run: a bounded 200 ms median sets its first note, and later note choices require an 18-cent preference plus 110 ms of continuous evidence. Confirmed changes keep their original onset. Short ornaments and ambiguous boundary notes favor stability; draw their targets manually when needed. This policy does not alter hand-authored points or Keep performance. An empty custom mask preserves measured targets. Parameter presets, including saved custom presets, leave manual curves intact. A portable effect chain saved with automation includes its pitch targets; a plain track/chain copy clears them while retaining the scoped insert's inactive mode.

## Applying to audio

The action states its scope before rendering:

- **Apply to audio:** for a first, scoped pitch stage, bake all of its edited regions together (including gaps between them), then remove that pitch insert. Other inserts and track mix automation remain live.
- **Apply full track:** for global pitch or a pitch stage later in the chain, bake the entire insert chain over the track and its capped tails, then remove those inserts. Gain, pan and mix automation remain live. This deliberate broader scope avoids processing the same effect twice or cutting a predecessor's small residual tail. A prefix-only experiment lost 1,440 tail samples despite matching all earlier audio; the implementation retains strict sample equality rather than hiding that discrepancy behind a tolerance.

The worker warms the same Mixer used by playback/export with absolute timeline automation clocks. It freezes resolved original clip envelopes before replacing audio and renders them exactly once. Source PCM remains available; one Undo/Redo includes the full chain and clip changes. Clip history recovers source audio, while project Undo restores effects too. Cancellation and changed audio, selection or session discard pending results without a history entry. Work runs off the main actor.

Full-track baking makes rendered tails visible as audio on the timeline. In multi-track sessions, conservative session-wide silent export padding can change when effect tails become clips; this does not establish universal identical export duration. Choose an explicit export range when a delivery must have an exact duration. Curve moves/copies use Move with automation, including split boundaries and cross-track moves. Ordinary audio-only moves leave timeline automation at its existing time.

## Persistence and limits

New projects use version 5. Version 1–4 projects remain readable; editing scoped targets upgrades their required version. Older Soundroom builds reject version 5 instead of silently dropping curves or changing scoped tuning into global tuning. Portable chains with scoped metadata use version 2; plain older chains remain version 1. Target/region IDs persist and are renewed when copying.

The detector measures a single pitch at 50–1000 Hz; synthesis correction remains limited to ±1200 cents. Low and raspy voices alternate pulse shapes, so a single 21 ms window can read an octave, a fifth or a twelfth away from the voice. The tracker keeps the period that continues the recent pitch while it explains the window about as well, accepts a jump that persists for six analyses (32 ms), and keeps following a breathy window that still repeats at the tracked period. Against a drawn curve, a target within 250 cents of an octave from the sung note is reached in the octave being sung rather than by an octave of correction. On five recorded baritone takes this reduced time spent shifting by more than 600 cents from 1.3–2.5% to 0.1–1.7%, and Tight-curve error from 52–93 to 18–58 cents RMS (`portable/crates/engine/tests/pitch_quality.rs`). A requested target outside that shift range can differ from measured output. Dense plots cull invisible samples and retain extrema and unvoiced separators. Playback prepares indexed curve cursors off the callback and uses 5 ms raised-cosine scope gates inside edited regions, with exact aligned dry audio outside them.
